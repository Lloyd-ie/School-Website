WHO WE ARE

Datamex College of Saint Adeline – Valenzuela is a private school that focuses on providing quality education, supporting students' growth, and preparing them for opportunities in a changing and competitive world.

02

Mission

DCSA is committed to providing affordable, innovative, and quality education to its students through highly qualified mentors, service-oriented personnel, modern facilities, and efficient procedures anchored on the values of integrity, selfless service, and excellence.

03

Vision

By 2026, DCSA is a PACUCOA-accredited college which develops academically competitive, socially responsible, productive, and Godly individuals who are locally and globally competent graduates.

04

These values guide students, teachers, and staff in building a positive and responsible school community.
