STEM · Grade 11–12

Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics

Prepares students for science, engineering, and health-related college courses.

What you'll learn

  • Advanced math and the sciences
  • Research and laboratory skills
  • Problem solving and critical thinking

Career paths

  • Engineer or architect
  • Scientist or researcher
  • Medical and health professional
×

ABM · Grade 11–12

Accountancy, Business and Management

Builds a strong foundation in accounting, business, and management for students aiming for careers in commerce and entrepreneurship.

What you'll learn

  • Fundamentals of accounting and finance
  • Business math and economics
  • Principles of management and marketing

Career paths

  • Accountant or auditor
  • Business owner or entrepreneur
  • Manager or marketing professional
×

HUMSS · Grade 11–12

Humanities and Social Sciences

For students who are curious about people, society, and culture. Prepares them for courses in education, law, communication, and the social sciences.

What you'll learn

  • Creative writing and communication
  • Society, politics, and culture
  • Community engagement and social issues

Career paths

  • Teacher or educator
  • Lawyer or public servant
  • Journalist, social worker, or psychologist
×

ICT · Grade 11–12

Information and Communications Technology

Gives students hands-on skills in computers, programming, and digital tools, ready for IT courses or entry-level tech work.

What you'll learn

  • Computer programming and web design
  • Computer systems and networking
  • Digital tools for everyday work

Career paths

  • Software or web developer
  • Network or systems technician
  • IT support or digital creator
×

BSIT · Bachelor's degree

Bachelor of Science in Information Technology

Develops skills in programming, systems, and networks to prepare graduates for careers in technology.

What you'll learn

  • Programming and software development
  • Databases and web systems
  • Networking and system administration

Career paths

  • Software or web developer
  • Systems or network administrator
  • IT support specialist
×

BSHM · Bachelor's degree

Bachelor of Science in Hospitality Management

Prepares students to work in hotels, restaurants, and tourism, with training in food service, operations, and customer care.

What you'll learn

  • Food and beverage preparation and service
  • Hotel and restaurant operations
  • Customer service and event management

Career paths

  • Hotel or restaurant manager
  • Chef or culinary professional
  • Event or tourism coordinator
×

BSOA · Bachelor's degree

Bachelor of Science in Office Administration

Trains students in office management, communication, and technology for administrative and support roles.

What you'll learn

  • Records and office management
  • Business communication and documentation
  • Office software and digital tools

Career paths

  • Administrative assistant
  • Executive or office secretary
  • Office or records manager
×

ACT · Associate course

Associate in Computer Technology

A focused program that builds practical computer skills for entry-level IT work or further study.

What you'll learn

  • Computer hardware and software basics
  • Programming fundamentals
  • Basic networking and troubleshooting

Career paths

  • Computer technician
  • Help desk or IT support staff
  • Junior programmer
